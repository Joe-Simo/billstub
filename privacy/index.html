Billstub

Privacy Policy

Billstub does not collect any data. Your bills stay on your iPhone.

Effective October 26, 2026.

What Billstub stores

Your bills, amounts, due dates, payment history, notes, and reminder settings are stored only on your device, in an App Group shared with Billstub's widgets. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Billstub does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No bank connection. Billstub never asks for bank, card, or account logins.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

CSV export

If you use CSV export (Pro), Billstub creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice.

Purchases

Billstub Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and reminders

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Billstub does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Billstub's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Billstub stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Billstub does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
