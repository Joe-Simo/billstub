Adding a bill

Tap + and enter the name, the amount (or turn on Amount Varies), the next due date, and how often it repeats: one time, weekly or every few weeks, twice a month, monthly or every few months, or yearly. A bill due on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months and returns to the 31st after.

Marking a bill paid

Swipe right on a bill and tap Paid, or open it and tap Mark Paid. To record a different amount or date, swipe left and tap Amount. You can undo right away, or open the bill and tap Undo Last Payment. Bills marked Autopay record their payment automatically once the due date passes.

Reminders

Billstub reminds you the day before each bill at 9:00 AM. With Billstub Pro you can choose how many days before, the time, and a second reminder on the due date. If reminders don't arrive, open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Billstub, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Widgets and Siri

With Billstub Pro, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Billstub. Tap Paid on the widget to mark the next bill paid. You can also say "What bills are due in Billstub".

Purchases

Billstub Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Pro isn't unlocked after buying, open Settings in Billstub and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Your data

Billstub keeps your bills and payment history only on your iPhone. It never connects to your bank and cannot pay bills for you. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the Privacy Policy.
